Privacy Policy

← Back to BrailleVision

Effective date: June 1, 2026

1. Overview

BrailleVision (“we,” “the Service”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what information is processed when you use the web application and API.

2. Information we process

  • Images and camera frames — When you scan or upload, image data is sent to the BrailleVision API for Braille detection. Images are processed in memory to produce text results and are not stored permanently by default.
  • Recognition output — Detected text, confidence scores, and optional debug overlays are returned to your browser for display and optional text-to-speech.
  • Technical logs — Hosting providers may collect standard server logs (IP address, request time, user agent) for security and operations.

3. What we do not collect by default

  • User accounts or passwords
  • Payment information
  • Persistent storage of uploaded images on BrailleVision servers (unless separately configured)

4. Camera and microphone

Camera access is requested only to capture Braille images for scanning. BrailleVision does not record or upload audio; text-to-speech runs locally in your browser where supported.

5. Third parties

If the Service is deployed on a third-party cloud host, that provider may process network traffic under its own privacy policy. No advertising trackers are built into the BrailleVision application code.

6. Children

The Service is intended for general assistive use. If you are under 13, use the Service only with permission from a parent or guardian.

7. Security

We use HTTPS where configured for deployments. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Your choices

  • Deny camera permission and use image upload only.
  • Disable voice guidance in accessibility settings.
  • Stop using the Service at any time.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest revision.

10. Contact

Privacy questions may be directed via the project repository: github.com/BoyTiger-1/BrailleVision.
