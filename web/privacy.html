Privacy Policy

← BrailleVision

Effective: June 1, 2026

1. Overview

BrailleVision processes images you capture or upload so we can detect Braille and return text. This policy explains what happens to that data.

2. What we process

  • Images — sent to the API for recognition; not stored long-term by default.
  • Results — text and confidence scores returned to your browser.
  • Server logs — standard request metadata may be kept by the hosting provider.

3. What we don’t collect by default

  • Accounts or passwords
  • Payment details
  • Advertising trackers in the app

4. Camera & speech

The camera is used only when you start a scan. Text-to-speech, when enabled, runs in your browser where supported—we do not upload audio.

5. Your choices

You can use uploads instead of the camera, turn off voice guidance, or stop using the service at any time.
